---
title: "Ravenous"
date: "2026-04-21"
description: "A poem about hunger, authenticity, and what we're really reaching for."
---

I want you terribly, it isn't something I have control over.

I pray that what I am starving for isn't a cardboard cutout of what you think I want, but it's the flesh and bone of what you are.

If it is, what a waste.

For everyone.
