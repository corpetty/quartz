---
title: "Broken Bird"
date: "2026-04-07"
description: "A poem about vulnerability and the stories we tell ourselves."
---

"aww, poor little bird," she thinks as she picks me up and gently holds me, careful to not do harm.

"He's injured," she thinks as she carresses my wing, softly to soothe me and not make things worse.

"How sad," she think as she sets me back to ground.

And then walks away.
