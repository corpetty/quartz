## 2. Legal Sources and Court Cases

### Supreme Court

- *Payton v. New York*, 445 U.S. 573 (1980) – Fourth Amendment warrant requirements
- *Zadvydas v. Davis*, 533 U.S. 678 (2001) – Detention time limits
- *Trump v. CASA* (June 2025) – Habeas corpus rights under Alien Enemies Act
- Roberts, Chief Justice John. Year-End Report on the Federal Judiciary, December 31, 2025.

### Federal Statutes

- U.S. Constitution, Article I, Section 9, Clause 2 – Suspension Clause
- Homeland Security Act of 2002, Public Law 107-296
- 8 U.S.C. § 1229a – Removal proceedings
- 18 U.S.C. § 242 – Deprivation of rights under color of law
- 5 U.S.C. § 2954 – Congressional oversight authority

### District Court Cases (2025-2026)

- *Souvannarath v. ICE*, M.D. La. – TRO violated, deportation to Laos
- *López Belloza v. ICE*, D. Mass. – Order violated, deportation to Honduras
- *Lunn v. ICE*, N.D. Ill. – Fourth Amendment violations, consent decree
- *Santiago v. ICE* – DACA recipient Fifth Amendment ruling
