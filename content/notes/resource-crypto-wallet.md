The wallet you should be using when playing with crypto will be a function of what you're doing. I'll define some things to do and popular wallets to use based on advice and opinions that have been given to me. 

Note: For anything Ethereum related and "normal everyday use", I use the [Status App](https://status.app). Why? Because I work at the [IFT](https://free.technology) and it is one of our projects. I am heavily biased and invested into it's success. Also I know that it is secure, both the chat and the wallet. It lacks the functionality that I use sometimes so I go to a different wallet for that.
